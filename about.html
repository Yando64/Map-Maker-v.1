About Tactical Map

Tactical Map is a static, client-side web application for creating and sharing military map overlays using standard NATO and US Army symbology. It runs entirely in your browser — no server, no login, no data uploaded anywhere. Overlays are saved as JSON files you control.

MIL-STD-2525D

MIL-STD-2525D is the US Department of Defense standard for common warfighting symbology. It defines how military units, equipment, installations, and control measures are represented on maps and displays. Symbols are organized by affiliation (friendly, enemy, neutral, unknown), echelon, and function.

Reference: MIL-STD-2525D (Joint Chiefs of Staff)

NATO APP-6

NATO Standardization Agreement (STANAG) APP-6 provides the Alliance equivalent of MIL-STD-2525, enabling interoperability among NATO member nations. The frame shapes and color conventions are shared; some internal icon geometry differs for specific unit types such as aviation and engineer symbols.

Reference: NATO Standardization Office (NSO)
